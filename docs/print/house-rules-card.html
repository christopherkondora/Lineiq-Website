The House Rules

The Beliefs inspire. The House Rules decide. When a decision is contested, these settle it. Each one names what it costs us to hold, because a principle that costs nothing is just a comfortable belief.

What we pay to hold it Audited quarterly by a named gardener
  1. 01

    Build the Compound, Never the Tax

    Cost of holdingWe turn away the large, easy market of clients who just want this month's campaign, and forgo the ~€1,500 to €4,000 per month of project and retainer volume that most of the competitor set lives on.

    Gardener: Áron

  2. 02

    We Refuse Leads

    Cost of holdingWe lose every prospect whose mental model is “I need more leads.” A real percentage of inbound will bounce on this exact word.

    Gardener: Kristóf

  3. 03

    Three Seats Only

    Cost of holdingWe leave real revenue on the table during cash-tight months, which is exactly where this rule will hurt first.

    Gardener: Áron

  4. 04

    Earned Pay, No Refunds

    Cost of holdingWe carry 90 days of unpaid delivery risk per pilot, and we walk away clean from misses we could have tried to salvage.

    Gardener: Áron

  5. 05

    Run It On Ourselves First

    Cost of holdingWe are slower to market. We cannot sell a service until we have run it on ourselves, and that delays revenue.

    Gardener: Kristóf

  6. 06

    Ship No Slop

    Cost of holdingWe publish less and slower than firms that mass-generate. We say no to easy volume.

    Gardener: Áron, copy. Creative Director, visual.

  7. 07

    One Channel, Well

    Cost of holdingWe are absent from channels where some of the audience is, and we watch competitors appear everywhere.

    Gardener: Áron

  8. 08

    The Wow Bar

    Cost of holdingWe rebuild work that is already good enough, spending hours others would bank.

    Gardener: Áron

  9. 09

    Disqualify Fast

    Cost of holdingWe disqualify roughly 60 to 70% of inbound, and walk away from prospects who could pay but do not fit.

    Gardener: Áron

  10. 10

    Maximum Contrast

    Cost of holdingContrast is slower and riskier than imitation. We discard safe, proven-looking work, and sometimes we get it wrong reaching for distinct.

    Gardener: Áron

  11. 11

    Clarity Is Currency

    Cost of holdingClarity is harder and slower to produce than complexity. Simplifying takes more drafts than dumping everything we know.

    Gardener: Áron

  12. 12

    Radical Honesty, in Plain View

    Cost of holdingWe lose prospects who wanted to be flattered, and we expose weaknesses competitors would hide.

    Gardener: Áron

The Plan-to-Break Clause. These are meant to be held under pressure. That is the entire point. They change only through a documented Keep, Refresh, or Retire decision with a named decision-maker, never by quiet erosion. A rule eroding without that decision means we have lost our spine, and that is the thing to fix.
